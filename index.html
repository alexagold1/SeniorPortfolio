About

Marlboro High School — 2023–2026

I design with passion,
build with precision.

Over these past three years in the FRHSD Web Design Pathway Program, I've developed a deep understanding of how design and technology intersect. I can't believe how quick the time has passed and how much I've grown from learning the basics of "what is the internet made of?" to professionally making websites for local businesses.

I create digital experiences that balance aesthetics, functionality, and usability. I've learned to care about the details others overlook: responsiveness, the emptiness of space, the difference between too many and not enough words.
